Synthetic vs Real-World Performance Disclosure

Synthetic Evaluation Performance: In this controlled prototype environment, synthetic fraud typologies (structuring, velocity bursts, spikes) follow mathematical distribution boundaries. Consequently, supervised algorithms (Random Forest) achieve near-perfect metrics (F1 ≥ 0.99).

Real-World Banking Challenges: In live production banking networks:
• Extreme class imbalance (fraud is often < 0.1% of transactions).
• Adversarial drift: fraudsters continually adapt tactics to evade known features.
• Label latency: confirmed fraud often takes weeks to be reported.
• Real-world production F1-scores typically range between 0.45 and 0.75.
